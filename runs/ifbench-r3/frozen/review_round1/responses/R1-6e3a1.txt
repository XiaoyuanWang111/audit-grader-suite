\[ U(P, f) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} c_i (x_i - x_{i-1}) \]

\[ L(P, f) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} c_{i-1} (x_i - x_{i-1}) \]

\[ U(P, f) - L(P, f) < \epsilon \]
